# 08 Research Entry Points Outline

## Goal

Turn the learning path into small, controlled, publishable research questions.

## Candidate Projects

- Edge VLA deployment on Apple Silicon.
- Action representation ablation on a small manipulation benchmark.
- Language grounding in small-data sim-to-real transfer.
- Auxiliary future prediction for ACT / SmolVLA-style policies.
- ACT vs Diffusion Policy vs SmolVLA under limited demonstrations.

## Minimum Experimental Discipline

- One baseline.
- One changed variable.
- Fixed seeds and datasets.
- Closed-loop evaluation.
- Failure mode taxonomy.
