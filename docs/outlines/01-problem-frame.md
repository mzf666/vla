# 01 Problem Frame Outline

## Goal

Prevent the reader from mixing together policy learning, world modeling, and VLA. The article should establish the five-question taxonomy used throughout the series.

## Core Diagram

`static/diagrams/vla-boundary-taxonomy.svg`

## Sections

1. Why terminology is confusing in VLA papers.
2. Policy: observation/history/goal to action.
3. World model: state/action to future state/reward/done.
4. VLA: language-conditioned embodied policy.
5. Overlap: when VLA includes future prediction.
6. Classification table.

## Key Insight

Do not classify methods by model name. Classify them by input, output, future prediction, language conditioning, and training objective.
